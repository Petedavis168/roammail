HOW-TO

Move from Gmail to Roammail

It takes about five minutes of your time. The copying happens in the background.

1. Create your Roammail address

Choose your address and password. Add a recovery email and turn on 2-step verification in Settings → Security; they’re your way back in.

2. Bring your Gmail across

Open Settings → Import mail, type your Gmail address and choose how far back to copy (for example the last 2 years). Google will ask you to allow it. Your mail keeps arriving in Gmail while it copies, and nothing is deleted there.

3. Tell people your new address

Send a quick note from Roammail to the people you email most. In Gmail, set up forwarding to your Roammail address so nothing gets missed while everyone catches up.

On your phone

Open Settings → Phone & apps in Roammail and scan the code. Your iPhone or Android sets up mail, calendar and contacts on its own. You can also install Roammail from your browser like an app.

Ready?

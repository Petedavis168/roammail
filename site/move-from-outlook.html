HOW-TO

Move from Outlook to Roammail

Personal or business, the steps are short. Copying runs in the background.

1. Create your account

For yourself, choose a free @roammail.net address. For a business, add your domain: we show you each DNS record and check it for you, or add them all in one click if your domain is on Cloudflare.

2. Bring your Outlook mail

Open Settings → Import mail, type your Outlook or Microsoft 365 address and choose how far back. Microsoft will ask you to allow it. Nothing is deleted from Outlook.

3. Keep the apps you like

Outlook on Windows or Mac, and the mail apps on your phone, can all connect to Roammail. Settings → Phone & apps sets them up for you.

Moving a whole company

Admins can import everyone at once from a spreadsheet, keep the same addresses, and turn on sign-in with Microsoft so people use the account they already know.

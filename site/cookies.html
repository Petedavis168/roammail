1. Cookies and similar storage

This notice covers cookies, local storage, and session storage used with the Roammail website. Our application code does not set browser cookies. It does use the device-storage items listed below. Hosting and ChatGPT sign-in may set separate security, access, or session cookies outside our application code; the exact infrastructure inventory should be confirmed before public launch.

2. Privacy preference — roammail-privacy-v1

First-party local storage. Records the policy version, whether you allow demo-message persistence, and the date of your choice. Used to apply your privacy preference rather than for analytics. Retention: up to 180 days, checked when the website loads. You may change it through Privacy settings or remove it by clearing browser site data.

3. Demo messages — roammail-demo-v1

Optional first-party local storage. Contains sample messages, starred state, and any recipients, subjects, or text you enter in the demo. Created only after you choose to remember demo messages. An accompanying roammail-demo-saved-at timestamp controls expiry. Retention: up to 30 days from the latest save, checked when loading the demo. Without permission, demo interactions operate in memory. Disabling saving removes stored demo messages.

4. Setup draft — roammail-setup

First-party session storage used when you request the setup flow. Contains your address choice and the details you enter: name, optional business name, username, recovery email, and domain. Retention: the tab’s browser session, subject to browser session restoration. Use Privacy settings to erase it sooner. The website does not send the draft to a live registration service.

5. Analytics, advertising, and replay

None are installed in this publication. There are no active Google Analytics or Google Ads tags, Microsoft Advertising UET tags, Microsoft Clarity scripts, advertising pixels, or session-replay tools. These categories cannot be enabled through the current preference panel. Adding a provider requires its identity, purpose, storage and retention details, and appropriate consent or opt-out handling to be configured first.

6. Making and changing choices

Use Privacy settings in the footer to change demo storage preferences or clear local demo and setup data. Rejecting optional persistence leaves the website usable. Clearing cookies or using another browser may require you to make the choice again. Browser settings can block or erase storage; some preview functions may then reset. Controls here cannot delete independent provider logs or sign-in cookies.

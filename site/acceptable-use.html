Acceptable Use Policy

Rules that protect people, domains, and the Roammail service.

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · Current website preview

1. Scope2. No spam or abusive messaging3. No harmful or unlawful content4. Respect identity and ownership5. Protect infrastructure6. Reports and enforcement7. Questions and reconsideration

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Roammail website and preview. It also describes intended conduct requirements for any future Roammail email service, subject to that service’s launch terms. No live mail service is activated by this policy.

2. No spam or abusive messaging

Do not use Roammail to originate or facilitate unsolicited bulk messages, deceptive marketing, phishing, scams, or mail intended to evade recipients’ choices. Any future commercial messaging must follow applicable law, identify the sender accurately, and honor required opt-outs. The current demo cannot deliver messages.

3. No harmful or unlawful content

Do not use the website for threats, harassment, unlawful discrimination, exploitation, child sexual abuse material, illegal transactions, malware, or material that violates another person’s legal rights. Do not encourage illegal acts or upload information you are prohibited from processing.

4. Respect identity and ownership

Do not impersonate a person or business, forge sender identity, use a domain without authority, infringe intellectual property, or misleadingly claim to represent Roammail or Friendly Web Services, Inc. Use only details you are entitled to provide.

5. Protect infrastructure

Do not attempt unauthorized access, password attacks, credential theft, exploitation, disruption, denial of service, circumvention of controls, or excessive automated requests. Security research must stay within applicable law and any written authorization. Do not access another person’s messages or information.

6. Reports and enforcement

Report abuse, rights concerns, or security issues to legal@friendlywebdesigns.com. Include enough context to locate the issue, but do not send passwords, exploit payloads, or unnecessary personal information. We may investigate, restrict access, preserve information where lawfully required, or cooperate with lawful process. Enforcement depends on the facts and applicable law; nothing promises that every report results in removal.

7. Questions and reconsideration

If you believe a restriction was mistaken, contact legal@friendlywebdesigns.com with relevant details. We will consider the information you provide. Privacy concerns should be sent to privacy@friendlywebdesigns.com.

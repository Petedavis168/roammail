Common questions

Is a Roammail account free?

Yes. The confirmed launch offer includes a free @roammail.net account with up to 20 GB of storage. Live account activation is not connected in this preview.

What do I need to use my own domain?

You’ll need to own the domain and have access to its DNS settings. Choose “Use my own domain” in setup for a guided checklist.

How much is email with my own domain?

Roammail Business is US$4 per mailbox/month with 50 GB per mailbox. Pay US$44 per mailbox/year for 12 months, saving US$4. Domain registration is separate. These are launch plans; this preview does not collect payment.

Which Friendly apps are included?

Both Free and Business launch plans include Friendlymeets, Friendly Docs, Friendly Sheets, Friendly Slides, and Friendly Notes. The 20 GB Free and 50 GB Business allowances shown here refer to mailbox storage. App-specific storage and usage limits will be detailed before launch. App access is not connected in this preview.

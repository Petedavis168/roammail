1. Who is responsible

Friendly Web Services, Inc., a Wyoming corporation, operates Roammail. Contact privacy@friendlywebdesigns.com for privacy questions and rights requests. This notice covers the website and setup preview. Live mailbox processing will require additional disclosures before launch.

2. Information in the preview

If you use setup, you may enter your name, optional business name, chosen username, recovery email, and domain. These details are stored in this tab’s session storage. If you compose a demo message, the recipient, subject, and message body are processed in your browser. Sample messages are fictional. The website code does not submit setup drafts or demo message content to a registration or mail server. Please avoid sensitive or confidential information in the preview.

3. Technical and hosting information

Loading the website requires our hosting and access providers to process network requests, which may include IP address, browser and device information, request times, requested URLs, and security events. The private preview uses ChatGPT sign-in and associated access controls. Those services may process account and session information under their own notices. We do not describe infrastructure logs as anonymous or claim they are absent. Exact provider retention and processing arrangements must be confirmed before live email launches.

4. Purposes and applicable legal bases

We use information to operate the functions you request, retain your privacy choices, assist with requests, maintain security, prevent abuse, and meet applicable obligations. Depending on the jurisdiction and activity, processing may rely on steps you request before a contract, performance of a contract, consent for optional device storage, legitimate interests in secure operation where applicable, or legal obligations. We will not treat acceptance of terms as blanket consent to optional tracking.

5. Device storage and optional tracking

Our Cookie Policy lists website-controlled storage. Optional saving of demo messages is off until you choose it. No Google Analytics, Google Ads tags, Microsoft Advertising UET tags, Microsoft Clarity scripts, advertising pixels, or session-replay tools are installed. Google and Microsoft tools are being considered, but are not activated by accepting storage preferences. We will identify any chosen products and providers, update disclosures, and add appropriate controls before activation.

6. Sharing and service providers

Website delivery and private access depend on hosting and sign-in providers. We may disclose information we actually hold when legally required, to protect rights and security, or in a lawful business transfer with appropriate notice and safeguards where required. The current website code does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or transmit setup drafts to advertising providers. These statements describe this publication and do not certify all processing performed independently by infrastructure providers.

7. Retention

Privacy preferences expire after 180 days unless renewed or cleared. With permission, demo messages are retained on this device for up to 30 days after the latest save; they are removed on a later page load after expiry or when you disable saving or clear them. Without permission, demo messages are kept only in page memory. Setup drafts remain in session storage until cleared or until the browser ends that session; restored browser sessions can preserve them. A downloaded setup file remains on your device until deleted. Provider logs have separate retention arrangements, which are not set by these browser controls.

8. Your choices and rights

Privacy settings lets you enable or disable demo-message persistence and erase locally stored demo messages and setup drafts. You can also clear site data in your browser. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, receive a copy of, restrict, or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a regulator. Contact privacy@friendlywebdesigns.com. We may request proportionate verification and will respond within applicable deadlines. We do not require you to submit a password or sensitive identification through this preview.

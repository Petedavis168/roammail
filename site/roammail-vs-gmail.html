Roammail vs Gmail

Gmail is a great product. Roammail is built for people and small businesses who want private, ad-free email and a team that answers when they need help.

The short version

  • No ads, ever. Roammail is paid for by Premium and business plans, not advertising.
  • Private between Roammail users. Email between two Roammail addresses never leaves our servers and shows a Private badge.
  • Built-in scam protection. Every attachment is checked, links that pretend to go somewhere else are flagged, and one tap reports a scam.
  • Your own domain (you@yourbusiness.com) with guided setup, including one-click DNS for domains on Cloudflare.
  • Bring everything with you. Import your Gmail in the background, as far back as you choose.

Features people switch for

  • Disappearing and password-protected emails
  • Follow-up reminders when nobody replies, and “Remind me” when you promise something
  • Catch up: get through a full inbox one email at a time
  • Out-of-office with different replies for your team and everyone else
  • Read and delivered receipts between Roammail users
  • Shared team inboxes with assigning, private team notes and “someone is replying”

What stays the same

Roammail works with the apps you already use: the iPhone and Android mail apps, Outlook and Thunderbird, plus your calendar and contacts. It also works in any browser and installs like an app.

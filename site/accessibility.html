Accessibility Statement

Our approach to making Roammail easier to use.

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · Current website preview

1. Our aim2. Current limits3. Report a barrier

1. Our aim

Friendly Web Services, Inc. aims to make the Roammail website usable with keyboards, assistive technology, and different screen sizes. We use semantic labels, visible focus states, text alternatives, and responsive layouts. These are design practices, not a certification of conformance.

2. Current limits

This evolving preview has not undergone a complete independent accessibility audit. We do not claim full WCAG compliance. We will assess reported barriers and prioritize practical improvements. The product preview and account setup remain in development.

3. Report a barrier

Email legal@friendlywebdesigns.com with the page, task, and problem you encountered. If helpful, include the browser or assistive technology used. Do not send private message content or sensitive information. Tell us what alternative format or assistance would help.
